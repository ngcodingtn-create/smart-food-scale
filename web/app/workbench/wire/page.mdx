# Wire it

Every wire in the build, one at a time. Tick each off as you make it; the ticks stay in this browser.

<WireIt />

Find a pin by the **name printed beside it** on your board, not by where it sits in the
drawing. The DevKit's order was checked against a reference; the ESP32-CAM's was not.
Wire colours are a suggestion.

## Before power goes on

<Callout type="danger" title="Read the metal shield on the DevKit">

If it says **WROVER**, not WROOM-32, GPIO 16 and 17 belong to its memory. Move the HX711
to **GPIO 25 and 26**. This one half-works: the board boots, the display runs, and the
weight readings are garbage.

</Callout>

<Callout type="danger" title="Nothing that idles high on the camera's GPIO 12">

The ESP32 reads GPIO 12 at boot to pick the flash voltage. Held high, the board does not
start, and it looks like a dead board. The light is on GPIO 13 for this reason.

</Callout>

<Callout type="caution" title="Find the transistor's legs before you plug it in">

2N2222s in a plastic case are sold in both leg orders, and a BC547 is the other way
round again. Read the datasheet for the marking on yours, or find the base with a
multimeter's diode test.

</Callout>

First power-up: the bench supply at 5.00 V with a 1 A current limit, so a wiring mistake
costs nothing.

| Pins | By |
| --- | --- |
| 0, 5, 18, 19, 21, 22, 23, 25, 26, 27, 32, 34, 35, 36, 39 | The camera |
| 16, 17 | Its memory |
| 2, 14, 15 | The microSD card, in 1-bit mode |
| 4 | The flash LED, kept as a fallback light |

With the card in 1-bit mode, `SD_MMC.begin("/sdcard", true)`, only GPIO 4, 12 and 13
come free.

## Power

A brownout mid-capture is a corrupt photo and a lost weight.

- **5 V, 2 A or more**, into the camera's 5V pin. Not 1 A.
- **1000 µF and 100 nF** across it, as close to the board as they will go.
- **Short, thick leads. No breadboard** in the camera's supply.
- **One lead from the supply to each board**, joined only at the supply, with the second 1000 µF there.
- **The HX711 on Node A's 3V3**, never on the camera's supply.

## HX711 settings

| Setting | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Channel | A | The only one with gain 128 |
| Gain | 128 | Matches the load cell's output |
| Rate | 10 samples a second | 80 is far noisier, and the plate is not moving |
| Averaging | Median of 10 to 16 | A median rejects a single wild sample; a mean does not |

What this buys is on [Accuracy](/reference/accuracy).
