# Test and calibrate

Node A, read live over USB. Every reading, every event, and a calibration you save to the board. No board yet? Practise.

<Bench />

## Check each part

With Node A flashed and wired, connected above:

| Part | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display | Power on | `CAMERA ?` on top, `NOT CALIBRATED` at the bottom, until it is calibrated |
| Load cell | Press the plate gently | The counts rise, then fall back when you let go. They fall instead? Swap white and green |
| TARE | Press it | One short beep, and `tare offset=…` in the log |
| PHOTO | Press it, once calibrated | `WEIGHING`, then two beeps and `SENT`, or one long low beep and `NO CAMERA` while Node B is off |
| Camera link | Find camera | `link found ch=…` once Node B runs; `link none` until then |

The camera and its light are checked with Node B, whose firmware is not written yet.

## About the calibration

A straight line through zero, fitted to every mass you record: tare first, so no load
means no counts. Five masses across the range, then a check with masses not used in the
fit, is what decision D13 asks for. Borrow the masses from a physics or chemistry lab
and weigh them on their balance first.

<Callout type="note" title="What practice mode is">

A pretend scale inside this page, speaking the same lines as the firmware. Its counts
per gram (100) and its noise are invented, so nothing measured in practice belongs on
the [accuracy page](/reference/accuracy).
