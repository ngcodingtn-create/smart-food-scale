## What "open" means

A stage is open when its page is built and works today. A waiting stage has no page
yet, because the thing it needs does not exist: there is no firmware beyond a build
file, no hosted backend, no app, and no parts on the bench. Nothing here is a mock-up.

Every outside tool, and where each one stops, is in the [Toolbox](/workbench/toolbox).
