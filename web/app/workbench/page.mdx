## What "open" means

A stage is open when its page is built and works today; partly open when it works for
Node A but not yet for Node B. A waiting stage has no page yet, because the thing it
needs does not exist: Node B's firmware, the hosted backend, the app, the parts on the
bench. Nothing here is a mock-up.

Every outside tool, and where each one stops, is in the [Toolbox](/workbench/toolbox).
