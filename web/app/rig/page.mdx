## How the assembly works

<div className="rig-assembly" id="rig-assembly-description">
  <p>The <strong>plate and circular platform</strong> sit above a horizontal straight-bar load cell. One end of the bar is bolted to the stationary base; the opposite end connects upward to the platform. A clearance gap keeps the plate and platform off the enclosure, so the food’s weight passes through the sensor.</p>
  <p>Inside the base, the <strong>HX711 amplifier</strong> reads the load cell and sends its digital value to the <strong>ESP32 DevKit V1</strong>. The front panel carries an <strong>SSD1306 OLED</strong> and separate tare and capture switches. A passive buzzer provides feedback. Tare the empty plate, add food, then capture the net mass.</p>
  <p>A separate <strong>fixed arm</strong> rises from the base behind the plate. At its tip, the <strong>AI-Thinker ESP32-CAM and OV2640 lens</strong> point vertically down at the plate’s centre. Twelve pixels in the <strong>WS2812B-compatible ring</strong> face the food around the lens. The arm’s weight is supported by the base, not by the weighing platform.</p>
  <p><strong>Node A sends grams to Node B over ESP-NOW</strong>, without a router between the boards. Node B pairs those grams with a JPEG and uploads both to the backend over Wi-Fi. No direct phone connection or router hardware is part of this model.</p>
</div>

<details className="rig-assumptions" id="assembly-assumptions">
  <summary>Assembly assumptions and modelling limits</summary>
  <p>The scene is authored in millimetres, converted once so one Three.js world unit equals one metre. The 250 × 240 mm base accommodates a Ø180 mm plate, a front control strip, a rear arm and separate electronics. A 350 mm overall height leaves 249 mm from the estimated lens face to the plate’s eating surface.</p>
  <p>The 20 mm arm section, platform, fasteners, enclosure walls, small board details, cables and material finishes are proposed geometry. They are not a fabrication specification. The exact board revisions, plate mass, sensor mounting, arm stiffness and camera field of view must be checked when hardware arrives. The light is illustrative; it is not a calibrated photometric or lens simulation.</p>
  <p>Exploded mode separates the assemblies vertically and moves the two base boards outward without scaling any part. Its extra spacing is illustrative, not a dimension to manufacture. The platform is attached only at the moving end of the cell; strain and deflection are not exaggerated.</p>
