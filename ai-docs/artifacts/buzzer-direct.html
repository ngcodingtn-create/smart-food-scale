Smart Food Scale · step 1.6, the buzzer

Does the buzzer itself work?

The click test gave no click, so either 5 V does not reach the buzzer, or the buzzer module does not work. Take the breadboard out of the question: two female–female jumpers from the module straight to the NodeMCU. All on the row next to the breadboard, by the USB. Drag to turn; scroll or pinch to zoom.

drag to turn · scroll to zoom

The two wires

S → 15th pinThe last pin, by the USB: 3V.
− → 14th pinThe pin before it: G. Push on, listen, pull off.

Counted on the NodeMCU’s row next to the breadboard, from the far end. A click when − touches G: the buzzer works, and the fault is the 5 V on the breadboard. No click: the buzzer module. Then put the module back where it was.
