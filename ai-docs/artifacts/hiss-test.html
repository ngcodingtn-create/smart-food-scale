Smart Food Scale · step 1.6, the buzzer

Where does the hiss come from?

A constant faint hiss means current flows through the buzzer all the time, so a beep has nothing to switch. Either the transistor is switched on all the time, or the buzzer’s − reaches ground without it. One move tells which: pull the transistor out and listen. Drag to turn; scroll or pinch to zoom.

What the hiss tells

Hiss stopsThe transistor was on all the time: its middle leg gets a pin that is high, or it sits in the wrong rows.
Hiss goes onThe buzzer’s − reaches ground without the transistor: a wire or a pin in the wrong row.

USB stays in, so you can hear it. Afterwards the transistor goes back exactly as it was: same three rows, flat face the same way.
