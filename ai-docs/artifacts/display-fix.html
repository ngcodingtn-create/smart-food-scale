Why the display stays dark

Nothing is plugged onto the display’s four pins, so the board cannot see it.

Claude restarted the board from the laptop. It says:

Also: your NodeMCU is upside down, pins pointing up. That is fine for plugging jumpers on, but its names are now underneath and its two rows of pins swap sides. The bench below and the map at the end show it the way you see it.
