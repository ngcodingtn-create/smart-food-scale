Is the transistor the right way round?

The buzzer stays silent when the transistor is back to front. Find its E leg by eye, check it is in the black wire’s row, and turn it round if not. Drag to turn the view; scroll or pinch to zoom.

The rule Look at the flat face, legs pointing down: the left leg is E. E’s row must hold the black wire that goes to the − rail. The blue wire, to the buzzer, is in C’s row, on the other side.
