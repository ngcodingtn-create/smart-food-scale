Is the diode the right way round?

Each beep made the board drop off USB: something joins 5 V to ground when the buzzer switches on. The usual cause is the diode turned the wrong way. Its black band must be in the S row, with the red wire. Drag to turn the view; scroll or pinch to zoom.

The rule The diode’s black band goes in the S row: the row of the buzzer’s S pin and the red wire to 5 V. Its plain end goes in the − row: the row of the buzzer’s − pin and the blue wire from the transistor. Band the other way, and each beep is a short circuit.
