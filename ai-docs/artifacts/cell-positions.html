Where does the cell bend?

The cell only reads when it bends: one end held still, the other end free. Four ways to try, A to D. After each, tell Claude: Claude reads the board while you press and says whether it moved. Drag to turn; scroll or pinch to zoom.

A · In your handsGrip the wired end; press the 20Kg ↓ end with your thumb. Proves the cell answers.
B · Label end outWired end held on the box, label end 3 cm out. Press the label end.
C · Turned roundLabel end held, wired end out. Press the wired end.
D · Upside downAs B, holes down, the arrow up.

Press for about 3 seconds, let go, then tell Claude which letter you just did. The right position: the counts move a lot while you press and come straight back when you let go.
