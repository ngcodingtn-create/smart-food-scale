Does the buzzer click?

One test that splits the problem in half: touch the buzzer’s − straight to ground and listen. A click means the buzzer and its 5 V are fine; no click means they are not. Drag to turn the view; scroll or pinch to zoom.

The rule Touching the buzzer’s − to ground does by hand what the transistor does on each beep. A passive buzzer only clicks on DC: that click is the answer. Put the wire back in the same hole afterwards.
